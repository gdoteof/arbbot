Fee model in force

what every post-fee number above assumes — and what the scenarios change
Kalshi — taker ceil¢(0.07·n·P·(1−P)), maker ceil¢(0.0175·n·P·(1−P)). Fixed schedule; no volume tiering on fees (maker programs exist but aren't modeled).
Polymarket (international) — taker k·n·P·(1−P), k by category (0 geopolitics · 0.04 politics/finance/tech · 0.05 sports/econ · 0.07 crypto), makers free. Venue-reported override: the CLOB marks some markets fee-free (taker_base_fee=0 — NEH, Putin-out class); the scanner honors that per market for new observations.
Scenarios — as scanned: fees exactly as charged at observation time. PM zero: every Polymarket taker leg free (upper bound if the fee-free set is wider than reported). 2× stress: all fees doubled (schedule-drift stress). Kalshi maker: Kalshi legs at the maker coefficient (the Stage-4 make-take world). Scenarios reprice the recorded baskets; observations counts don't change.
